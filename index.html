How to Play

Goal

Form a 4x4 grid by matching the edges of the puzzle pieces.

Matching Maths

Two edges match if they describe the same square. You might need to match different properties of that square:

  • Side Length (e.g., Side = 10 cm)
  • Perimeter (e.g., 4 × 10 = 40 cm)
  • Area (e.g., 10 × 10 = 100 cm²)

Example: An edge showing "Side 10 cm" will connect to an edge showing "Perimeter 40 cm".

Color Hints

The colors represent units. You will always connect edges of the same color.

  • Blue = Millimetres (mm)
  • Red = Centimetres (cm)
  • Green = Metres (m)
  • Yellow = Kilometres (km)

Controls

  • Drag pieces to move them.
  • Click a piece to select it.
  • Spacebar or Double-Click to rotate the selected piece.
